Location

Located in central Hokkaido, the Tomamu Resort, Village Alpha offers unbeatable ski-in, ski-out access and walking distance to everything you need.

  • Ski lifts & slopes – 5 min (ski in, ski out)
  • Hotaru Street – 5 min walk
  • Resort Centre – 5 min walk
  • Mina-Mina Beach (Onsen) – 7 min walk
  • Ice Village (Winter) – 7 min walk
  • Tomamu The Tower – 10 min walk
  • New Chitose Airport – 90 min drive

Getting There

From New Chitose Airport

By Coach

During the ski season, ski buses connect Tomamu directly with Sapporo's Shin-Chitose Airport (120 minutes, 6000-yen one way, reservations required, departures every 2 hours). Less frequent buses connect Tomamu to central Sapporo, Niseko, Rusutsu, Furano and Asahikawa.

By Train

JR limited express trains run every 2-3 hours between Sapporo and Tomamu stations. The one-way trip takes around 100 minutes, costs 5,880 yen and is fully covered by the Japan Rail Pass and Hokkaido Rail Pass. Seat reservations are mandatory.
